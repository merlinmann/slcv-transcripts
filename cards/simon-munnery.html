Simon Munnery is the comedy genius.

Lee's word, not mine. Used more than once.

  • Lee calls him "legendary," "always apposite," "the comedy genius" — and admits most of his best jokes are written by, inspired by, or bought off Munnery.
  • Together they ran Cluub Zarathustra, a mid-nineties Dadaist cabaret so confrontational Munnery once built a weapon for it called the CUNT Ray, aimed at the audience.
  • Munnery's one-line parody of observational comedy — "Anyone ever noticed anything, ever?" — is the kind of thing Lee quotes in his book with obvious envy.
