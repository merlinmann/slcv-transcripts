Ted Chippington made Stewart Lee.

One gig in Birmingham, 1984. Everything after.

  • Sixteen-year-old Lee sees Ted open for The Fall — a Teddy Boy in a monotone, telling the same joke about walking down a road, over and over, until the form itself falls apart.
  • That night Lee decides: stand-up can be anything. You don't even have to look like you're enjoying it.
  • Lee dedicated his book How I Escaped My Certain Fate to Ted. First page. Two words. No explanation.
