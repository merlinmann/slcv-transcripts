Chris Morris is his creative peer.

The shadowy satirist. The only interviewer who could sit across from him.

  • When the BBC let Lee hire a fake interviewer to frame his stand-up on TV, he picked Morris — twice — for two full seasons of Stewart Lee's Comedy Vehicle.
  • Morris doesn't lob softballs. He asks Lee if he's shitting on his own doorstep, if he sounds like a mad old pope, if he's just a traffic warden for laughter. Lee brightens every time.
  • In the book, Lee calls Morris "the shadowy satirist" and credits him with planting "infant" — one of the great comedy words.
