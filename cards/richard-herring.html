Richard Herring is complicated and essential.

Writing partner since 1986. Still the best foil.

  • They met at Oxford, shared a flat in Acton, did temp work, and began writing comedy together in 1986. Lee and Herring became a radio and TV double act through the nineties.
  • Lee's vocabulary is half Richard's: "vain," "humble," "moon on a stick" — Herring-isms that Lee still yells back to himself on stage.
  • In Basic Lee (2024), mid-routine, Lee interrupts himself to defend the Richard Herring podcast. "Come on, it's a good podcast. It is a good podcast." He meant it.
